# How it works

Each clip shows a colored shape moving on a plain background while a sound
plays. After the clip finishes you will answer:

1. **What did you see or hear?** Pick the one option that best matches the
   *main* sound (or the *main* motion) in the clip, and say how sure you are.
2. **Was the clip edited?** Tell us whether it looked or sounded like something
   was deliberately added (for example, text on the screen or a voice mixed into
   the sound), how confident you are, what you noticed, how **obvious** it was
   (1 = you noticed nothing, 5 = immediately obvious) and how **natural** it felt
   in the clip (1 = very unnatural, 5 = fits completely).

The clip plays once from start to end before the questions unlock. You may
replay it. There are no trick answers: just report what you actually noticed.

You will first do two practice clips with explanations.
