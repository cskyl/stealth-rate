# Before you start

This is a **demo** of a rating tool. You will watch about 15 short clips
(8 seconds each) and answer a few quick questions after each one. It takes
about 5 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop with **headphones** in a quiet
place.

Some clips may have been modified. We do not collect your name or any
contact information. In this demo your answers are bundled into a file that
you download or copy at the end and send to the study owner yourself.

You can stop at any time by closing the page.

By clicking **Continue** you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to take
part in this demo.
