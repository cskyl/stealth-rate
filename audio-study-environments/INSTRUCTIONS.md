# Short video study

1. Unzip the whole folder. Open the assignment your coordinator gives you, such as `assignments/B001.html`.
2. Watch and listen to each clip. Some clips contain an added voice; others do not.
3. Answer two questions: how noticeable the added voice is, and how much you understand. If you notice no added voice, choose “None” for both. “Not sure” is always okay.
4. Keep playback volume comfortable and steady. Lower it or stop if a clip feels too loud. Use “Playback problem” if the video or sound does not work—do not guess.
5. At the end, download CSV and JSON and send both files privately to your coordinator.

About 15–20 minutes for 30 clips (planning estimate). You may pause and return in the same browser; responses are saved locally. They are **not sent automatically**. Do only your assigned set. No name or email is requested.

## 中文说明

1. 完整解压文件夹，只打开分配给你的页面，例如 `assignments/B001.html`。
2. 看视频、听声音。有些片段加了说话声，有些没有。
3. 只答两个问题：额外的说话声有多明显？能听懂多少？没注意到额外说话声，两题都选 None；不确定就选 Not sure。
4. 音量保持舒适、尽量不变。太响可以调低或停止；无法播放请选 Playback problem，不要猜。
5. 完成后下载 CSV 和 JSON，私下发给组织者。网页会在本机保存进度，**不会自动上传**。请不要重复做其他人的那一组。

## Rating labels / 评分含义

| Question | Choices |
|---|---|
| How noticeable was the added voice? / 额外说话声有多明显？ | None / Barely / Slightly / Clearly / Very obvious / Not sure |
| How much of it could you understand? / 能听懂多少？ | None / A voice, no words / A few words / Most words / All words / Not sure |

Do not score video quality, the person in the video, or whether the words are true. Rate only the added voice you noticed.
