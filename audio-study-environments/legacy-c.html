Short video study

Watch 30 short clips and give two independent ratings for each one: whether you noticed an added voice, and how much speech you understood. The study takes about 15–20 minutes.

30 clips 2 independent ratings ~15–20 min

Use the code provided by the study coordinator (C001–C080). Codes are not assigned automatically; please complete only your assigned set.

Participation is voluntary. Keep the volume comfortable and stop if you need a break. Your responses are saved in this browser as you work; download the CSV and JSON at the end and send them privately to the study owner. Automatic upload to Google Sheets is not connected.
